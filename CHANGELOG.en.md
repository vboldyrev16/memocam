## 0.2.2 — 2026-09-10

- Camera selection in the main view and compact call controls, saved device choice, refresh, and disconnect handling.
- A local preview mirror toggle and a separate clean outgoing stream preview.
- Center-cropped camera output filling 1280×720 instead of adding side bars for a 4:3 source.
- Head-turn guards for squint and nose-bridge gestures, a narrower bridge target, and no cached face for that gesture.
- Calibration preserves absolute eye-closure scores so its baseline cannot hide a blink.
- Completed waves no longer require an extra hold, tolerate one bent finger, and can supersede a preceding open-palm reaction.
- Importable author presets. The shipped preset is empty; the author's personal selection is not included yet.
- Camera, output, selection, and squirrel playback regressions included in CI.
